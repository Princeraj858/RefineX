⚡ AI Energy Saving Recommendations
🔩
Steam Trap Audit — Utilities Section
AI detected 3 steam traps with abnormal acoustic signatures likely indicating open-failure. Immediate audit recommended to prevent continuous steam blowthrough.
Estimated Saving: ₹18L/month
💡
Off-Peak Shift of Non-Critical Loads
Several auxiliary pumps and lighting circuits can be shifted to 02:00–06:00 off-peak window. Grid tariff difference could yield significant savings without impacting operations.
Estimated Saving: ₹12L/month
🌀
Variable Frequency Drives for Cooling Pumps
Cooling water pumps E-701 and E-702 run at fixed speed. Installing VFDs to match actual demand could reduce electricity consumption by 20–30% on these units.
Estimated Saving: ₹9L/month
🔥
Furnace Air-to-Fuel Ratio Optimisation
CDU furnace excess air percentage is running at 18% vs recommended 12–14%. Tightening the ratio can reduce fuel gas consumption without affecting outlet temperature targets.
Estimated Saving: ₹22L/month
